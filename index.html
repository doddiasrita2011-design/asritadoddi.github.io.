Biography & Research Focus

I'm a researcher working at the intersection of biomedical engineering and applied machine learning. My work centers on building tools that make healthcare technology more precise, more transparent, and more trustworthy — whether that's an algorithm reading a chest X-ray or a sensor tracking a patient long after they've left the clinic.
